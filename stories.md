# The World in One Day — Monday 23 November 2026

The stories on the front page (entry.png), as researched and cross-checked

## Lead story

**Ukraine says its sea drones sank a Russian warship in Libya** — President
Volodymyr Zelensky confirmed on Monday that seven Ukrainian-made naval
drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury in Benghazi harbour, destroying a
Ka-27 helicopter on board, and hit the cargo ship MV Angara, which Kyiv
says carried weapons for Russia. The attack, at dawn on Sunday, took place
about 1,050 miles from Ukraine's coast. Libya condemned the strike, and
the United Nations called for an independent investigation.
*Source note: reported by Reuters, CNN, The Times, Le Monde, the BBC and
the Associated Press.*

## Story 2

**Nvidia brings back Jensen Huang — and the markets roar** — Nvidia's board
reinstated Huang as chief executive on Monday, 11 days after he resigned,
after President Trump intervened and officials confirmed his return posed
no national security risk. Nvidia shares closed up 31.5%, their best day
since 2003, adding nearly $1 trillion in value; the Nasdaq rose 6.5% and
the S&P 500 rose 4.7%.
*Source note: reported by Reuters, the Wall Street Journal, CNN and the BBC.*

## Story 3

**Russian strikes kill at least 15 around Kyiv** — Overnight strikes on the
Kyiv region hit a block of flats in Dymer, killing 11 people in one
building, and a supermarket in Vyshneve. Rescuers were still digging
through the rubble on Monday, and the region declared a day of mourning.
*Source note: reported by the BBC, Reuters and Ukrainian officials.*

## Story 4

**NATO meets over Russian missiles that entered Poland** — NATO's North
Atlantic Council met in Brussels on Monday to discuss Russian missiles
that crossed into Polish airspace on Friday during a huge Russian barrage
on Ukraine. Poland called the incursion a deliberate provocation; no
injuries or damage were reported in Poland.
*Source note: reported by Reuters, the BBC and Polish officials.*

## Story 5

**Indonesia confirms 24 dead in factory fire** — A fire at a confectionery
factory in Banten province, west of Jakarta, killed at least 24 people,
with about 30 still missing. The toll was confirmed on Monday; the blaze
broke out on Saturday evening and may have been caused by an
air-conditioner short circuit.
*Source note: reported by Reuters and Indonesia's presidential labour adviser.*

## Story 6

**NHS begins the world's biggest AI breast-screening trial** — The NHS
launched the EDITH trial on Monday: nearly 700,000 women at 30 screening
sites over three years will test whether AI can help specialists spot
breast cancer earlier. The first recruits joined in Cambridgeshire.
*Source note: reported by NHS England and the BBC.*
